Prices are observed from publicly visible retailer listings. Pack sizes are normalised where the retailer discloses them. Listings are refreshed on a weekly cycle; the Last seen date on each card indicates the most recent successful observation.

Note: not every retailer publishes a pack size in a structured field. Where the pack size appears only in the product title, downstream consumers are expected to parse it themselves.
